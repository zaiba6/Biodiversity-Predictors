Discussion

Key interpretations

  • Regularized regression performs strongest on current reported metrics, indicating strong linear structure after feature prep.
  • Random forest captures nonlinear interactions with moderate error and stable CV behavior.
  • GAM provides interpretable smooth-effect views even when predictive fit is lower than competing methods.
  • Slider outputs are sensitivity scenarios around notebook baselines, shown for interactive exploration.
